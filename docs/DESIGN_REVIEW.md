# Visual review — existing 80 invitations

2026-11-26, Chrome desktop at 390×780 with reduced motion and fonts ready. Fresh opening and opened captures were produced for every ID before and after the targeted change. Eight contact sheets were inspected visually, followed by full-size Paper Cut and Tiny Footsteps openings/details. Opened details share the existing invitation engine. This is a composition review, not a claim that every intermediate frame of every animation received artistic approval. Automated interactive opening coverage complements it.

## Findings by flow

1. **Browse and choose:** 80 IDs remain in eight groups of ten. The gallery now reads lightweight metadata, keeps existing tiles when filtering and prioritizes visible thumbnails. See [matrix](TEMPLATE_REVIEW_MATRIX.md). Screenshot hashes are inventory evidence only.
2. **Opening composition:** Paper Cut had oversized overlapping sheets and insufficient breathing room. Full-size inspection showed crowding, rather than actual viewport clipping. Sheet scales were reduced to 1.62–1.66, the leaf to 1.05, and a small original glint balances the left margin. The existing sheet/leaf vocabulary, palette, layer actions, ID and all saved invitation records remain intact. Its static cover was regenerated. [Before](design/paper-cut-before.webp) / [after](design/paper-cut-after.webp).
3. **Category art direction:** General has stationery, deco, glass and ticket layouts; Ramadan uses moon/lantern/arches; Fitr gifts/floral/celebration; Hajj architectural/geometric/travel motifs; Adha gathering/lantern/gift motifs; weddings envelopes/rings/botanical scenes; baby cloud/cradle/animal themes; graduation caps/diploma/stairs/frames. No glass effect was imposed on these vector templates. Tiny Footsteps is deliberately sparse; no speculative embellishment was applied.
4. **Readable reveal:** Full-size opened samples preserve shaped Arabic, RTL, long-form date, seconds and timezone, countdown and venue sections. Existing mobile suites exercise widths 360–1440, signup/editor/photo/date/publishing and guest actions. Fonts remain licensed and self-hosted. Capturing reduced-motion stills does not establish animation smoothness; measured rAF/interaction regressions are disclosed in PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md.
5. **Comparison with references:** The existing reference-video review remains in docs and test-results/reference-review. Current templates use layered vector stationery, while the videos include photographic material, dimensional props and richer scene staging. This pass did not produce new video frame captures or prove equivalent luxury. No reference design was copied. Further artistic refinement needs a selected direction and motion/device review, not hash uniqueness or a blanket replacement of 80 working templates.
